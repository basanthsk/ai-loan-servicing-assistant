heading. The id in square brackets is what the assistant cites.

## [KB-C-01] Changing the billing cycle
The cardholder can change the statement date at least once, through a service request
in the app. The new cycle starts from the next statement; the statement in between may
cover a shorter or longer period. The due date moves with the statement date.
Source: RBI Master Direction - Credit Card and Debit Card - Issuance and Conduct (2022).

## [KB-C-02] When a late payment fee applies
A late payment fee and interest on the overdue amount apply only when the payment is
overdue for more than 3 days after the due date. Paying at least the minimum amount due
within that time avoids the late fee, but interest still applies to any unpaid balance.
Source: RBI Master Direction - Credit Card and Debit Card - Issuance and Conduct (2022).

## [KB-C-03] Closing a credit card
A card can be closed by a request in the app, by email or by phone once all dues are
paid. The closure is completed within 7 working days. If it is not, the bank pays a
penalty of Rs 500 a day of delay until the account is closed. Unused reward points
should be redeemed before closure.
Source: RBI Master Direction - Credit Card and Debit Card - Issuance and Conduct (2022).

## [KB-C-04] Credit limit changes
The credit limit is never increased without the cardholder's explicit consent. A limit
increase can be requested in the app and is subject to an eligibility check. The
cardholder can also reduce the limit at any time.
Source: RBI Master Direction - Credit Card and Debit Card - Issuance and Conduct (2022).

## [KB-C-05] Liability for unauthorised transactions
If an unauthorised transaction is reported within 3 working days of the alert, the
cardholder's liability is zero. Reported within 4 to 7 working days, the liability is
limited to the transaction value or Rs 10,000, whichever is lower. Report at once and
block the card to stop further misuse.
Source: RBI circular on customer liability in unauthorised electronic banking
transactions (2017).

## [KB-C-06] Reward points
Reward points are earned at 2 points per Rs 150 spent on retail purchases. Fuel, rent,
wallet loads and EMI transactions do not earn points. Points are valid for 3 years and
can be redeemed for statement credit at 25 paise a point or for vouchers in the app.

## [KB-C-07] Converting a purchase into EMIs
A purchase of Rs 2,500 or more can be converted into EMIs of 3, 6, 9 or 12 months
within 30 days of the transaction, before the statement due date. A one-time processing
fee of 1% (minimum Rs 199) plus GST applies, and the interest rate is shown before the
customer confirms. Converting a purchase blocks that amount from the credit limit until
it is repaid.

## [KB-C-08] International and online usage
Online, contactless and international transactions can be switched on or off, and
spending limits set for each, from the app at any time. A foreign currency markup of
3.5% of the transaction value plus GST applies to international transactions.

## [KB-C-09] Cash withdrawals
Cash can be withdrawn at ATMs up to the cash limit shown on the statement. A cash
advance fee of 2.5% (minimum Rs 500) applies, and interest is charged from the day of
withdrawal until it is repaid in full: there is no interest-free period on cash.

## [KB-C-10] Complaints and escalation
A complaint about a card can be raised in the app, by email or by phone, and is
acknowledged with a reference number. If it is not resolved within 30 days, or the
customer is not satisfied with the reply, the customer can escalate to the bank's
Principal Nodal Officer and then complain to the RBI Ombudsman under the Integrated
Ombudsman Scheme through cms.rbi.org.in.
Source: Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.

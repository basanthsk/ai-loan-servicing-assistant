Policy knowledge base documents are split into one retrievable section per `##`
heading. The id in square brackets is what the assistant cites.

## [KB-L-01] Changing the EMI due date
The EMI date can be moved once a year, to the 5th, 10th or 15th of the month, through
a service request. The change applies from the second EMI after the request. Interest
for the extra or fewer days between the old and new dates is added to or reduced from
the first EMI on the new date. Any EMI that has already been presented to the bank is
not affected.

## [KB-L-02] Changing the bank account or auto-debit mandate
To pay EMIs from a different bank account, register a new NACH e-mandate from the app
or website using net banking or a debit card of the new account. Registration takes up
to 5 working days. Keep the old account funded until the new mandate shows as active,
because the next EMI is still debited from the old mandate until then.

## [KB-L-03] Ways to pay an EMI manually
If the auto-debit fails or the mandate is not yet active, the EMI can be paid by UPI,
net banking or a debit card from the app's "Pay now" option. A manual payment made
before the lender retries the debit stops the retry. Cash and cheques are not accepted
for personal loans.

## [KB-L-04] Loan statements and interest certificates
A statement of account for any period and the current repayment schedule can be
requested at any time and are sent by email within one working day, free of charge.
A provisional interest certificate for the financial year is available from January,
and the final certificate after 31 March. Interest on a personal loan is normally not
tax deductible unless the money was used for a home, business or education purpose.

## [KB-L-05] Key Facts Statement and the all-in cost
Every loan comes with a Key Facts Statement (KFS) that shows the annual percentage
rate (APR), the repayment schedule and all fees and charges. No charge that is not
listed in the KFS can be collected without the borrower's consent. A copy of the KFS
can be requested at any time.
Source: RBI circular on the Key Facts Statement for loans and advances (April 2024).

## [KB-L-06] Options when a floating rate is reset
When the benchmark rate changes and the loan rate is reset, the borrower may choose to
increase the EMI, extend the tenure, or a combination of both, within the maximum
tenure allowed by the product. At the reset the borrower may also switch to a fixed
rate once during the tenure; the switch fee is shown in the KFS. The lender tells the
borrower about every reset and its effect on the EMI and tenure.
Source: RBI circular on reset of floating interest rate on equated monthly instalment
based personal loans (August 2023).

## [KB-L-07] Penal charges for late payment
Late or failed payments attract a fixed penal charge, never penal interest added to
the loan rate. Penal charges are not capitalised: no further interest is charged on
them. The amounts are listed in the KFS and in the schedule of charges.
Source: RBI circular on fair lending practice - penal charges in loan accounts (2023).

## [KB-L-08] Credit bureau reporting
Repayments are reported to the credit bureaus every month. An EMI paid late is
reported as overdue for the days it was late, even if it was paid in the same month.
A failed debit that is paid within the retry window before the reporting date is not
reported as overdue. Corrections to bureau data are made within 21 days of a valid
dispute.

## [KB-L-09] Documents after the loan is closed
When the loan is fully repaid or foreclosed, the no-dues certificate is emailed within
7 working days and the auto-debit mandate is cancelled automatically. The credit bureaus
are updated in the next monthly report. Post-dated cheques or security cheques, if any,
are returned or destroyed on request.

## [KB-L-10] Top-up loans and loan restructuring
A top-up loan can be offered after 12 EMIs have been paid on time, subject to an
eligibility check. Customers facing temporary financial difficulty can ask for a review
of their repayment plan; each request is assessed case by case and any change is
reported to the credit bureaus as required.

## [KB-L-11] Complaints and escalation
A complaint can be raised in the app, by email or at a branch, and is acknowledged
with a reference number. If it is not resolved within 30 days, or the customer is not
satisfied with the reply, the customer can escalate to the bank's Principal Nodal
Officer and then complain to the RBI Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme
through cms.rbi.org.in.
Source: Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.

## [KB-L-12] Fraud and phishing warnings
The lender never asks for an OTP, PIN, password or card CVV over a call, SMS or email,
and never asks a customer to pay an EMI into a personal account or by a payment link
shared on a messaging app. Suspected fraud should be reported immediately so the
account can be protected.
